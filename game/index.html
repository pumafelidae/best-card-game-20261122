EMBER & TIDE

Duel of Three Winds — a card duel against the Warden

Ember Tide Thorn
  1. Each turn, play one card to clash with the Warden's card. The higher total wins — value, plus 2 when your element beats theirs.
  2. Ember beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Ember. Playing the same element twice in a row adds 2 more.
  3. The Warden shows its next element before you play — read it and counter it. Cards cost energy; energy grows one a turn. Pass to bank energy, but the Warden strikes while you hold back.
  4. Reduce the Warden to 0 life to win. Lose yours, and the winds keep the table.
